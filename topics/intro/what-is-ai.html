What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of machines or computers to behave in a way that appears "intelligent."

It means a machine can:

  • Learn
  • Think logically
  • Make decisions
  • Understand patterns
  • Solve problems
  • Improve from experience

Just like humans use their brain to make decisions, AI systems try to simulate some human intelligence using data and algorithms.

Real-Life Analogy

Imagine teaching a small child how to identify a cat. You show:

  • 1 cat
  • 10 cats
  • 100 cats

Eventually the child understands: "Animals with whiskers, ears, tail, and certain shape are cats."

AI learns similarly:

  • We feed data
  • AI finds patterns
  • AI makes predictions / decisions

Formal Definition
Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science focused on building systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks include:

  • Vision
  • Speech recognition
  • Language understanding
  • Decision making
  • Prediction
  • Planning
  • Creativity

Important Understanding

⚠️
AI does NOT "think" like humans
AI finds patterns, learns from data, and makes predictions mathematically. It does not have emotions, consciousness, or human understanding. Even advanced AI is still pattern prediction at a massive scale.
